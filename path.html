The prompting path

Five stops. Each one is a kind of sentence. Skip ahead and the later pictures will fail for a reason you cannot name yet.

  1. 01 · Craft · 4 min

    The grammar of a panel

    Learn the slots. Until a single panel is a sentence — subject, shot, angle, light, empty space — a sequence is only a pile of themes.
  2. 02 · Craft · 4 min

    One face, twelve pages

    Write a lock you paste unchanged. Geometry, a hair silhouette, clothes as objects. Stop improving it mid-scene.
  3. 03 · Craft · 4 min

    Screentone is a decision

    Gray is not a filter. Decide where it goes, how heavy it is, and the edge where it stops.
  4. 04 · Craft · 4 min

    Leave the gutter empty

    Prompt one panel. Keep lettering, speed lines, and the next panel out of the picture. The cut is the blank you do not draw.
  5. 05 · Method · 5 min

    A one-shot in twelve lines

    Put the earlier essays to work. One premise, one lock, one place, nine beats. Generate in that order, and stop when the row can be read without captions.
