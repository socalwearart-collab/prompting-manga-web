September 2026 · The prompting path

Write the page before the line.

A journal about the sentences that become panels. Shot, lock, tone, gutter — then a short path that turns twelve lines into a scene.

Start the path Read the lead essay

How to read

In order. The path is the issue. Each essay assumes the one before it, and the last one only works if the first four stuck.

One slot at a time. When a picture comes back wrong, change the slot the essay names. Do not answer a failure by adding adjectives.

Words stay out of the picture. The specimens leave paper blank on purpose. Set speech and sound effects afterwards, in type you can spell.

In this issue

Five essays. One sitting, if you want it that way.

  1. 01 The grammar of a panel Shot, angle, light, and the part you leave empty. Craft · 4 min
  2. 02 One face, twelve pages A character lock is a paragraph you stop rewriting. Craft · 4 min
  3. 03 Screentone is a decision Where the gray goes, how heavy, and where it stops. Craft · 4 min
  4. 04 Leave the gutter empty Prompt the panel. Keep words, effects, and the next panel out. Craft · 4 min
  5. 05 A one-shot in twelve lines The path itself: a scene that fits on one sheet. Method · 5 min
